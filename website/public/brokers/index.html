What it does

  • Asks the six things you would. Buy or rent, budget, area, BHK, timeline and home loan, one question at a time.
  • Works from your listings only. You keep them in a Google Sheet. It is told never to guess a price.
  • Hands over to you. Hot leads, complaints, and questions your listings don't answer come to your WhatsApp.
  • Stops when asked. If a buyer opts out, it doesn't message them again.

What it doesn't do yet

  • Answer voice notes
  • Reply in Marathi
  • Book visits in your calendar
  • Chase buyers who go quiet

Leads from Facebook forms, 99acres, MagicBricks and Housing.com are still being tested.

Questions brokers ask

Does my own WhatsApp number change?

No. The pilot runs on a separate number, for one ad.

Will buyers know it's a bot?

If they ask, it says it is your agency's automated assistant and that you can take over.

What if it gets something wrong?

It can make mistakes, so we review pilot chats daily. Prices in its replies are checked against your listings, and any mismatch is flagged to us.

Who sees my leads?

You do, and we do while running the service. We don't share them with other brokers. The privacy policy has the details.

What happens after the pilot?

You decide. We agree a monthly price with you first, and nothing is charged unless you say yes.
