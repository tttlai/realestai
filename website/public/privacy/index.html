Privacy Policy

Effective date: 16 November 2026

Realest AI ("we", "us") builds AI tools for businesses. One of our services is a WhatsApp assistant for real estate brokers in India: when someone enquires about a property, the assistant replies on the broker's WhatsApp Business number, asks a few questions about what they are looking for, and helps book a site visit.

This policy explains what personal data we handle, why, who we share it with, and the choices you have. It covers our website, realestai.in, and the WhatsApp assistant.

Our role

When you message a broker who uses our assistant, or submit an enquiry that reaches them, the broker decides how your enquiry is handled. We process your data on the broker's behalf to provide the assistant. For our own website and our relationship with brokers, we are responsible for the data ourselves.

Data we collect

If you enquire about a property

  • Contact details: your phone number and WhatsApp profile name, and your name if you give it.
  • Messages: the messages you exchange with the broker's WhatsApp number, including text and any voice notes or images you send, with their timestamps.
  • Property requirements: details you share in the conversation, such as whether you want to buy or rent, budget, preferred area, number of bedrooms, timeline and whether you need a home loan.
  • Site visits: the date and time of any visit you book.
  • Enquiry source: where your enquiry came from, such as a Facebook or Instagram lead form, a property portal (for example 99acres, MagicBricks or Housing.com), the broker's website or a direct WhatsApp message, and the property you asked about.

If you are a broker using our service

  • Your name, agency name, WhatsApp Business account details, the phone number for lead alerts, calendar details and property listings you give us.
  • Access tokens that let the assistant send messages from your WhatsApp Business number. These are stored encrypted.

If you visit our website

Our website does not use cookies, analytics or advertising trackers, and has no forms. Like any website, our hosting provider and the services that serve its images receive your IP address and browser details when you load a page.

How we use it

  • To reply to your enquiry on the broker's behalf and answer questions about the broker's listed properties.
  • To record your requirements so the broker can follow up with suitable properties.
  • To book site visits and send visit confirmations and reminders.
  • To alert the broker when you are ready to talk to them or need a person to help.
  • To send up to two follow-up messages if a conversation goes quiet, using WhatsApp message templates.
  • To keep a record of conversations and of the assistant's decisions, so a broker can see why the assistant said something and we can fix problems.
  • To keep the service secure and working.

We do not sell personal data, use it for advertising, or use your messages to train AI models.

AI processing

Replies are written by an AI model (Anthropic's Claude) using your messages, the broker's property listings and the details you have already shared. The assistant only discusses property enquiries and site visits for that broker, and is told not to make up prices or property details. Voice notes are converted to text by a speech-to-text provider so the assistant can understand them. You can ask to speak to the broker at any time.

Who we share it with

We share personal data only with:

  • The broker you enquired with, who can see your conversation and details.
  • Service providers that run parts of the service for us, under contracts that limit how they use the data:
    • Meta Platforms (WhatsApp Business Platform), to send and receive WhatsApp messages and receive lead form enquiries
    • Supabase, to store data
    • Railway, to host the application
    • Anthropic, to generate replies
    • a speech-to-text provider (OpenAI or Sarvam AI), to transcribe voice notes
    • Google, for site-visit calendar bookings
    • an email processing provider, to read enquiry emails forwarded from property portals
  • Authorities, where the law requires it.

Some of these providers process data outside India. We use reputable providers and only share what each needs to do its job.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiry and conversation data for as long as the broker uses our service and needs it to follow up on enquiries. When a broker leaves our service, or when you ask us to delete your data, we delete it from our systems within 30 days, unless the law requires us to keep it longer.

Stopping messages

Reply STOP (or say you are not interested, in any language) to stop all further messages from that broker's assistant. We record your choice and do not message you again.

Your rights

Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, and other applicable law, you can ask us to:

  • tell you what personal data we hold about you and how it is used
  • correct or update it
  • delete it (see how to request deletion)
  • withdraw your consent to further processing

Email tttlaiagency@gmail.com with the phone number you used. We may ask you to confirm the request from that number. We aim to reply within 30 days. If you are not satisfied with our response, you can complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

Security

Data is encrypted in transit. Brokers' WhatsApp access tokens are encrypted at rest. Access to personal data is limited to what is needed to run and support the service. No system is perfectly secure, and we will notify affected people and the authorities of a data breach as the law requires.

Children

Our service is for adults enquiring about property. We do not knowingly collect data from anyone under 18.

Changes to this policy
