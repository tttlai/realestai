Data Deletion

Last updated: 16 November 2026

You can ask us to delete the personal data we hold about you, including WhatsApp conversations handled by our assistant for real estate brokers. See our Privacy Policy for what we collect and why.

How to request deletion

  1. Email tttlaiagency@gmail.com with the subject Data deletion request.
  2. Include the phone number you used on WhatsApp (with country code), and the broker or agency name if you know it.
  3. We may ask you to confirm the request by sending a WhatsApp message from that number, so that nobody else can delete your data.

What happens next

  • We confirm we have received your request.
  • Within 30 days, we delete your contact details, messages, property requirements and site visit bookings from our systems, and email you to confirm.
  • If the law requires us to keep some data for longer, we tell you what and why, and delete it when that period ends.
Only want the messages to stop? Reply STOP to the broker's WhatsApp number. The assistant will not message you again. This stops messages but does not delete your data; email us as above for that.

Data held by others

Deleting your data from our systems does not delete copies held by others. The broker you enquired with may keep their own records, and WhatsApp keeps messages on your phone and handles its own data under WhatsApp's privacy policy. Contact them directly for those.

Facebook Login

Our app does not use Facebook Login and does not receive data from your Facebook account. If you sent an enquiry through a Facebook or Instagram lead form, follow the steps above to delete it from our systems.
